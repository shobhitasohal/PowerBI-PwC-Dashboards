# 📊 Power BI Dashboards | PwC Virtual Internship

A collection of three Power BI dashboards developed as part of the **PwC Internship Program**, designed to deliver actionable insights across customer service, retention, and workforce diversity.

---

## 📁 Dashboards in This Repository

| Dashboard | Business Focus |
|---|---|
| 📞 Call Center Performance | Service efficiency & agent performance |
| 🔄 Customer Churn Analysis | Retention risk & churn drivers |
| 🌍 Diversity & Inclusion | Workforce equity & gender balance |

---

## 📞 Dashboard 1 — Call Center Performance

### 🔍 Business Problem
Call centre managers lacked a centralized view of operations, making it difficult to monitor agent performance, identify bottlenecks, and improve customer satisfaction in real time.

### 🛠️ How It Was Solved
Built an interactive Power BI dashboard tracking key operational KPIs including call volume trends, average handle time, first call resolution rates, agent-level performance, and customer satisfaction scores.

### 📊 Output
- Identified peak call periods to support staffing decisions
- Ranked agents by performance to guide coaching and recognition
- Monitored satisfaction trends to flag service quality issues early

---

## 🔄 Dashboard 2 — Customer Churn Analysis

### 🔍 Business Problem
A telecom company was losing customers without a clear understanding of who was churning, why they were leaving, or which customers were most at risk, making proactive retention impossible.

### 🛠️ How It Was Solved
Analyzed customer demographic, contract, and usage data to identify churn patterns. Segmented customers by risk level and surfaced the key drivers behind attrition including contract type, tenure, and service usage.

### 📊 Output
- Identified high-risk customer segments for targeted retention campaigns
- Highlighted key churn drivers (month-to-month contracts, low tenure)
- Enabled proactive outreach before customers decided to leave

---

## 🌍 Dashboard 3 — Diversity & Inclusion

### 🔍 Business Problem
An organization struggling with gender imbalance at the executive level needed data to understand where diversity gaps existed across hiring, promotions, turnover and why progress was stalling.

### 🛠️ How It Was Solved
Analyzed HR data across gender-related KPIs including hiring rates, promotion rates, turnover, performance ratings, and executive pipeline by gender. Identified root causes of gender imbalance at senior levels.

### 📊 Output
- Pinpointed stages in the talent pipeline where gender gaps widened
- Surfaced disparities in promotion rates between male and female employees
- Provided data-backed recommendations to support DEI strategy
